About Jubizon Logistics

Connecting Nigerian Entrepreneurs Directly to China's Manufacturing Powerhouse.

Headquartered in Lagos with trusted fulfillment warehouses in Guangzhou and Yiwu, Jubizon Logistics is your end-to-end partner for commercial goods procurement and international freight forwarding.

We eliminate communication hurdles, supplier scams, currency exchange complexities, and cumbersome customs regulations.
